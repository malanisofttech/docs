04

Advertising & Ad Partner Rules (2026)

Our advertising practices comply with the IAB TCF v2.2, Google Play Policy 2026, Apple App Store Advertising Guidelines, and applicable national ad-tech regulations.

4.1 Types of Ads We Show

  • Contextual ads — shown based on content, not personal profiling (no consent required)
  • Personalized / interest-based ads — shown only with your explicit opt-in consent
  • Rewarded ads — optional video ads in exchange for in-app rewards
  • Interstitial & banner ads — displayed at appropriate intervals per platform guidelines

4.2 Advertising Partners

We work with third-party advertising networks. Each partner has its own privacy policy. Where applicable, you can opt out of personalized advertising through their opt-out mechanisms:

  • Google AdMob — policies.google.com/privacy
  • Meta Audience Network — facebook.com/about/privacy
  • Unity Ads — unity.com/legal/privacy-policy
  • AppLovin — applovin.com/privacy
  • IronSource / Unity LevelPlay — subject to Unity's privacy policy

4.3 Your Advertising Choices

  • You can opt out of personalized ads via iOS Settings → Privacy → Tracking or Android Settings → Google → Ads
  • You can reset your Advertising ID at any time in your device settings
  • Non-personalized (contextual) ads may still appear after opting out
  • Upgrade to a paid/premium version (if available) to remove ads entirely

4.4 Ad Data Usage Rules

  • We never use health, financial, or sensitive-category data to target ads
  • We never show behaviorally targeted ads to users identified as under 13
  • We comply with the EU Digital Services Act (DSA) ad transparency requirements
  • All ad impression data is used only for measurement and fraud prevention

Data Retention

We retain personal data only as long as necessary for the purposes stated in this policy or as required by law.

  • Active accounts: data retained for the duration of your use of the Services
  • Deleted accounts: data anonymized or deleted within 90 days of account deletion request, except where legal obligations require longer retention
  • Advertising data: aggregated, anonymized; event-level logs deleted after 13 months

  • Backup systems: residual copies may persist for up to 30 days in encrypted backups before being purged

Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your personal data against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction:

  • TLS/SSL encryption for all data in transit
  • AES-256 encryption for data at rest
  • Access controls with role-based permissions and least-privilege principles
  • Regular security audits and vulnerability assessments
  • Incident response procedures; in the event of a breach, affected users will be notified within 72 hours as required by GDPR and applicable law
  • Two-factor authentication for administrative access
No system is 100% secure. We encourage you to use strong, unique passwords and to notify us immediately at malanisofttech@gmail.com if you suspect any unauthorized access.
